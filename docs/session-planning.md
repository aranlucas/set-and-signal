# Planning with equipment and time

Open **Plan for equipment & time** from Plan or the workout chooser. Select a
source routine, the available equipment, a time budget and rest between sets.
The planner takes a snapshot of the source when selected; subsequent edits to
the source do not silently replace the draft.

The reviewed alternative list groups general movement patterns and names every
required machine, bench, rack or anchor. Available originals take priority.
Unknown exercises and exhausted alternatives are shown as omissions. Suggestions
can be rejected or replaced; sets and targets are editable. Loads, speeds and
per-side flags are not transferred to a different exercise. The list does not
infer medical restrictions or use an AI service.

**Fit to time budget** reduces sets, then cardio duration, then omits exercises
from the end. All changes remain visible against the original targets. Estimates
include five minutes preparation, one minute setup per exercise, three seconds
per rep, and the selected rest between sets. They are approximate; additional
work, warm-up, pace and rest change actual duration. Invalid, empty, duplicate or
over-budget copies cannot be saved.

**Save separate copy** adds a routine without changing the source, weekly plan,
active session or logged history. Choose the copy through the normal workout
flow when ready. Its explicit targets and chosen rests take priority over prior
history. Progression and supersets are disabled during adaptation. Normal routine
editing remains available; later edits can change the duration.

The accepted original/alternative snapshot is retained in the saved routine,
unfinished session and completed history, including through backup and typed Go
state mutations. Plan-share files continue to contain only the resulting program.
**Undo save** removes only an unchanged, unused and unscheduled copy. Planning
drafts resume in account-scoped device storage; they are not uploaded or synced.
Storage failure is shown and cannot publish a rejected copy.

Verification uses synthetic histories, guest/demo mode and the in-process
Convex test backend. It does not start real users' workouts, call hosted training
providers, or transmit personal histories.
